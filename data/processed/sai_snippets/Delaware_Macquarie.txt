Procedure PROXY VOTING AGENT VanEck has engaged Glass, Lewis & Co., LLC ("Glass Lewis"), an independent third party proxy voting specialist, to assist in the implementation and administration of proxy voting-related functions. Glass Lewis is responsible for notifying VanEck of all upcoming meetings, providing a proxy analysis and vote recommendation for each proposal, verifying that all proxies are received, submitting vote instructions to the appropriate tabulator, and contacting custodian banks to request missing proxies. In addition, Glass Lewis is responsible for maintaining copies of all proxy statements received by issuers and to promptly provide such materials to VanEck upon request.
