Method

  1. Read every N-PX vote filed by each family’s trusts and keep four judgment categories: shareholder proposals, contested director elections, M&A and executive pay.
  2. Management followers first: a family voting with management on at least 90% of 50+ items is Tier 3.
  3. Answer key: ISS and Glass Lewis recommendations quoted in companies’ and activists’ proxy materials ({{s.issQuoted}} ISS, {{s.glQuoted}} Glass Lewis items).
  4. Infer ISS’s call on {{s.issImputed}} more items from about 1,500 reference funds’ votes (Bayes method of Zytnick 2025). Used only if it recovers at least 90% of held-out quotes where ISS opposed the board: it scored {{s.issGate}}.
  5. A family agreeing with its adviser on at least 90% of items where the adviser opposed the board is Tier 2. Everyone else is Tier 1.

Limits

  • Mutual funds only. N-PX does not cover pensions or separate accounts.
  • One proxy year. The quoted answer key leans toward proxy fights.
  • Glass Lewis inference failed its check ({{s.glGate}} vs 90%), so Glass Lewis calls rest on quoted items only. Russell, SEI and PGIM were scored against Glass Lewis; treat those three calls as unconfirmed.
  • {{s.issSetAside}} ISS quotes were set aside by a quote audit; without it the ISS check does not pass.
  • These results predate a fix for trusts that file more than one N-PX report. A rerun is needed to confirm them.
